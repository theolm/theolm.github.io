Neko Caller

Neko Caller is a paid application that plays curated cat-attracting sounds and lets you photograph the cat with an in-app camera.

Data collection

Neko Caller does not collect, transmit, or store personal data. It does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or in-app purchases, and it requires no account or network connection.

Camera and photo library

  • The app requests camera access so you can frame and capture a photo of a cat.
  • The app requests permission to add photos to your device photo library so captured photos are saved directly to your gallery.
  • Camera and photo-library data are used exclusively on your device. Photos are saved to your device gallery and are never transmitted to us or anyone else.

Audio

All Sounds are bundled with the app. No audio is uploaded or recorded; the app only plays the bundled Sounds through your device speakers.

Third-party services

Neko Caller does not integrate third-party services that receive your data.

Children's privacy

Neko Caller does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated.

For privacy questions, contact the developer at me@theolm.dev.
